Roadmap

Follow the chapters in order to finish the story without locking yourself out of a single trophy.

  1. 1

    Prologue & Chapter 1

    Free-roam basics, first safehouses. Nothing missable yet — take your time.

  2. 2

    Chapter 2 — Vice City

    Start side activities early. Two strand missions become unavailable after the mid-point heist.

  3. 3

    Chapter 3 — The Keys

    Collectible sweep window. Coastal spawns despawn once the storm arc begins.

  4. 4

    Chapter 4 — Leonida Backcountry

    Heist prep and crew loyalty choices. Pick the crew you need for the gold-medal run.

  5. 5

    Endgame & Cleanup

    Free-roam cleanup, gold medals, and the platinum.

Missable warning

6 trophies can be permanently lost. Side missions “Bait & Switch” and “Last Call” expire when Chapter 3 ends, and the Keys collectible set despawns during the storm arc. Clear everything marked missable before advancing the story.
